Better Minimap Stutter Fix

Better Minimap, another author's minimap mod, checks every 2 seconds whether the map has changed, all in one frame. On a large map that is a visible hitch every 2 seconds, also while the game is paused. This companion makes the check run every 30 seconds and once you stop building, without changing Better Minimap.

Measured in a 195-minute session on a large map with the minimap open: Better Minimap alone checks the map 30 times a minute, about 5,850 times in that session. With the fix it checked twice a minute while nothing was built and three to eight times in minutes with building: about 490 checks in all.

Map Check Interval

Setting: Map Check Interval · 30 s by default

Why

While its window is open, Better Minimap checks every 2 seconds whether the map has changed, and goes through the whole map in a single frame. On a large map that takes 40 to 60 milliseconds, a visible hitch every 2 seconds.

What it does

Makes that check run every 30 seconds instead: 10, 30 or 60 seconds, or Better Minimap's own 2 seconds, in the mod's Settings. Towns growing and new industries appear at the next check, and the town markers' colours follow the same interval.

Good to know

  • Each check takes as long as before; it just comes far less often. Making the check itself cheaper is up to Better Minimap's author.
  • Better Minimap's longer pauses when it redraws the map after terrain changes stay as they are; the fix adds none.
  • It can be removed at any time.

Refresh After Building

Setting: Refresh After Building · On by default

Why

With a check only every 30 seconds, a road or station you have just built could take half a minute to show on the minimap.

What it does

Better Minimap checks the map once you stop building or bulldozing for a moment, so it shows your own changes. A burst of clicks with the track tool or the bulldozer is a single check, and while you keep building the map is checked at most every 10 seconds.

Good to know

  • Turned off, your building shows at the next timed check.

Settings

Choosing a save's mods: the gear next to Better Minimap Stutter Fix → Settings. Each save keeps its own choice. Off turns a feature off completely.

Compatibility

Needs Better Minimap. Without it, or with a version of it that checks differently, the fix does nothing. Better Minimap itself is not changed.
